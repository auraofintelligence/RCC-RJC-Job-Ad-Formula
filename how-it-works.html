How it works

From an existing ad or a few short answers to the right sections for each posting form.

A few facts from you.
Clear sections from your AI.

Use the builder to prepare one brief. Give it to the AI you already use, then review the result and copy each section into the right posting form.

  1. 01

    Start with what you have

    Already have a job ad? Paste it into the first box. Your AI will reuse its facts. Add only changes or extra information below.

    Starting fresh? Add the job title, location, hours, duties and other details you know. Short answers are fine. Leave undecided details blank.

  2. 02

    Choose where it will go

    Tick SEEK, LinkedIn, Facebook or a short notice. For LinkedIn, choose a job listing or a feed post. They use different fields.

    Choose a voice and include only benefits you can genuinely offer. Every voice uses Australian English, professional formatting and no em dashes.

  3. 03

    Give the brief to your AI

    Select Download AI brief. The .md file is a text document containing your vacancy facts and writing instructions. The visible Markdown content viewer shows exactly what is in it.

    Open a new chat in your usual AI, attach the file and send:

    Use this brief to write the selected fields.

    If your AI cannot accept the file, select Copy brief for AI and paste the text into the chat instead. File support depends on the AI and your account.

  4. 04

    Review, then copy and paste

    Your AI should return labelled sections for the destinations you chose, with platform selections kept separate from public wording.

    Check every fact, resolve visible placeholders and verify pay and conditions. Open the posting platform, paste each section into its matching field and make the required selections yourself.

    The employer reviews and publishes the ad. See the posting guides for the field details and recorded research limits.

The voice

Joyful, responsible abundance

A warm invitation, clear agreements and genuine opportunity. Describe the real work, support and benefits. Positive language earns trust when the facts support it.

Your answers stay on this device

The builder saves your draft in this browser. You choose when to download or copy it and share it with your AI. Use vacancy facts and approved public contact details.

Keep the final review human

Your AI can make mistakes. Missing details, conflicting facts and employment checks still need your attention. The builder prepares a brief; it does not submit listings or assess applicants.
